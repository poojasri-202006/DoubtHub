Web Development

What is the difference between localStorage and sessionStorage?

Course code: WD101 • 3 answers
Answer 1 · 8 likes

localStorage keeps data until it is manually removed. sessionStorage keeps data for the current browser tab session.

Answer 2 · 6 likes

Both store data as strings, but their lifetimes are different. Use JSON.stringify() to store objects.

Database Management

What is the difference between a primary key and a candidate key?

Course code: DB102 • 1 answer
Answer · 4 likes

A candidate key can uniquely identify a row. One candidate key is selected as the primary key.
